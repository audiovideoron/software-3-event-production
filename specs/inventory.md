# Inventory Spec — AV Gear

This is the catalog of equipment we own and the standard ways we deploy it. When an event spec calls for "two flanking screens" or "main PA," the model should pull from here.

## Screens

| Qty | Size (H × W) | Aspect | Use |
|-----|--------------|--------|-----|
| 2   | 8' × 14'     | ~16:9  | Large-format keynote / IMAG |
| 2   | 6' × 10'     | ~16:10 | Smaller sessions / breakouts / split-room |

_Convention note: sizes are written height × width. Bottom-of-screen typical mount height is ~3–4' off the floor for sightline clearance. In a 13'9" ceiling room (Minnesota Valley), the 8' × 14' screen tops out around 11–12' — workable, but the room cannot accept anything taller._

## Projectors

The deployed pairing of projector brightness to screen size determines the standard throw distance. These are the calibrated pairings we use:

| Projector | Target screen | Throw distance | Implied throw ratio |
|-----------|---------------|----------------|---------------------|
| 5,000 lm  | 8' × 14'      | 15'            | ~1.07 (standard zoom) |
| 7,000 lm  | 8' × 14'      | 12'            | ~0.86 (short zoom)    |
| 5,000 lm  | 6' × 10'      | 10'            | ~1.00 (standard zoom) |

_All projectors are floor / tower-mounted (ground-support only — Minnesota Valley has no rigging points). Projector raise height should bring the lens to roughly screen-center; on ground support that means a tower stack of ~5–6' for the 8' × 14' screens._

## What This Inventory Implies About What We Can Run

- **One stage with two screens** — we have two of the large-format and two of the smaller. Cannot simultaneously serve a main session plus a fully equipped breakout that also needs flanking IMAG.
- **Keynote / spoken-word formats** — Qu-16's 16 mic inputs are plenty for panels, speakers, lavs, and a few stereo playback returns. Music acts with more than ~12 channels start to crowd the desk.
- **No flown anything** — all speaker hang heights are limited by tower / stand height, with the ceiling capping us at ~11–12'.
- **House signal** — when at Minnesota Valley, the room's permanent SDI + XLR snake drop terminates at the FOH position; the Qu-16 plugs straight in there.
