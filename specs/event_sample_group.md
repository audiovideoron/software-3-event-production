# Event Spec — Sample Group

## Overview

80-person classroom session in **Ballrooms 1 and 2** of the Minnesota Valley Ballroom.

## Active Space

Bays 1 and 2 of the four-bay ballroom. The airwall between bays 2 and 3 is **closed**; the airwall between bays 1 and 2 is **open**. Effective room: ~59 ft wide × 60 ft deep.

## Stage

**12 ft wide × 8 ft deep**, built from two 6'×8' decks placed side by side. Centered on the south wall within the active 2-bay span (so center of stage sits at ~29.5 ft from the west wall).

## Seating

**Classroom style, 80 attendees.** Following the Minnesota Valley convention (back-to-back paired tables with chair-back tick markers), roughly three columns of paired tables across, four to five rows deep, plus a center aisle.

## AV (pulled from inventory.md)

- **Two 6' × 10' screens** flanking the stage
- **Two Tower 1000 speakers** at the downstage offstage lip (front fill)
- **FOH tech position** at the house SDI + XLR snake drop (north wall top-of-room)
- Mixer: Allen & Heath Qu-16 at FOH
- Projector pairing for the 6' × 10' screens: **5,000 lm projector at 10 ft throw** (standard zoom)

## What This Event Does Not Use

- Tower 3000 mains (event is small enough that front fills carry the room alone)
- K12s
- Larger 8' × 14' screens
- Pipe and drape (optional — depends on whether service doors need masking)

## Assumptions Flagged for Confirmation

- **Which bay houses the FOH snake drop?** Placing FOH at the top of bay 2 (closer to stage center) until confirmed.
- **Lectern / lavalier?** Assuming yes, on stage right.
- **Center aisle?** Assuming yes, splitting the classroom seating.
- **Service-side pipe and drape?** Assuming no for an internal/private event; would add for higher-production sessions.
